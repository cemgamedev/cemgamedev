# Privacy Policy – Dirty Garage Chaos VR

**Totos Games Technologies** · Effective date: 7 November 2026 · [Türkçe metin aşağıda](#gizlilik-politikası--dirty-garage-chaos-vr)

> **In short:** Dirty Garage Chaos VR is a single-player, offline game for Meta Quest 3 and Quest 3S. It has no account,
> no login, no chat or other social features, no ads and no analytics, and we do not run any server for it. We do not
> collect, sell or share your personal data. Your game progress stays on your headset.

## 1. Who we are

This policy covers the app **Dirty Garage Chaos VR** (package `com.totosgames.dirtygaragechaosvr`), published on the Meta
Horizon Store by **Totos Games Technologies**, Turkey ("Totos Games", "we", "us").

Contact for all privacy questions and requests: **cemdemirtas57@gmail.com**

## 2. What data the app processes

### 2.1 Ownership check with Meta Horizon

When the game starts, it asks the Meta Horizon platform (Meta Platform SDK) whether the Meta account signed in on the
headset owns the game. The app only receives a yes/no answer; if the answer is no, the game closes. The app does **not**
request or receive your user ID, name, user name, profile photo, e-mail address, age group, friends or followers list,
avatar, location, voice, camera images or payment information.

### 2.2 Data stored on your headset

The game saves your progress (in-game money, unlocked items, completed jobs) and your settings (for example comfort,
turning and language options) in a file in the app's private storage on your headset. This file never leaves your device:
the app does not upload it, and Android backup is turned off for the app.

### 2.3 Data processed by Meta and Unity

Your purchase of the game, your Meta account and the headset itself are handled by Meta under the
[Meta Privacy Policy](https://www.meta.com/legal/privacy-policy/). The game is built with the Unity engine; the engine may
send anonymous technical crash diagnostics (such as device model, operating system version and error details) to Unity
Technologies to improve the engine, under the [Unity Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy). This data does
not contain your name or your Meta account, and we do not receive it.

## 3. How the data is used

- The ownership check is used only to make sure the game runs for people who bought it (a Meta Horizon Store requirement).
- The save file is used only to let you continue your game where you left it.

We do not use any data for advertising, profiling, tracking or analytics, and we do not sell or rent data.

## 4. Sharing

We do not share any user data with anyone, and we do not use data processors or service providers for this app. We would
only disclose information if we were legally required to; since we hold no personal data about players of this app, there
is nothing on our side to disclose.

## 5. Children

Dirty Garage Chaos VR is intended for teens and adults (13+). We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone,
including children. The game has no chat, no communication with other people, no ads, no external links and no real-money
purchases inside the game. If you are a parent or guardian and have any question, please contact us.

## 6. How long data is kept

- The ownership result exists only in the headset's memory while the game is running.
- The save file stays on your headset until you start a new game or uninstall the app.
- We (Totos Games) do not keep any player data.

## 7. How to delete your data

Every user, in every country, can have their data deleted free of charge:

- **In the game:** open *Settings* and choose *New Game* to erase your saved progress.
- **On the headset:** uninstall Dirty Garage Chaos VR (Library, then the app's menu, then *Uninstall*). This deletes the
  save file, the settings and everything else the app stored on the device.
- **By e-mail:** write to **cemdemirtas57@gmail.com** with the subject "Data deletion – Dirty Garage Chaos VR". We answer
  within 30 days. Because we do not store any player data on our side, we will confirm this to you and help you remove the
  data kept on your headset.

Data that Meta or Unity hold under their own privacy policies (section 2.3) is not in our possession, so we cannot delete it
ourselves; you can ask Meta through your Meta account's privacy settings, and Unity through the contact options in its
privacy policy. If you ask us, we will tell you how.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR, the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) or
US state laws), you may have the right to ask what data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to object to its use
and to complain to a data protection authority. Contact us to use any of these rights; we will not charge you.

## 9. Security

The app does not send player data to us, so there is no server or database of players to protect. The save file is kept in
the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read.

## 10. Changes to this policy

If the app starts using data in a new way, we will update this page and the effective date above before the new version of
the app is released.

## 11. Contact

Website: [tolgakalaycioglu.com/totosgame](https://tolgakalaycioglu.com/totosgame/)
